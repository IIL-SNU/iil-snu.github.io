Imaging Intelligence Laboratory

서울대학교 지능형 이미징 연구실

We develop optical imaging instruments and the algorithms that reconstruct their images. Current work includes Fourier ptychographic and polarization microscopes that measure quantitative phase and birefringence, virtual staining of unstained tissue slides, lensless cameras with fabricated phase masks, and rolling-shutter speckle imaging of blood flow. The laboratory is part of the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Seoul National University and is led by Prof. Seung Ah Lee.

Principal investigator

Seung Ah Lee (이승아)

Seung Ah Lee is an Associate Professor at Seoul National University working on optical imaging systems and computational imaging: microscopes, cameras and optical metrology and inspection instruments designed jointly with optics and AI-based image reconstruction. She received her B.S. in Electrical Engineering and M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Seoul National University and her Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from Caltech, and was a faculty member in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Yonsei University (2018–2025).
