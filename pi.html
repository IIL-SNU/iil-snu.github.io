Biography

Seung Ah Lee is an Associate Professor at Seoul National University working on optical imaging systems and computational imaging: microscopes, cameras and optical metrology and inspection instruments designed jointly with optics and AI-based image reconstruction. She received her B.S. in Electrical Engineering and M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Seoul National University and her Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from Caltech, and was a faculty member in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Yonsei University (2018–2025).

Her Ph.D. work at Caltech (2014) was on chip-scale lensless microscopy. She was a postdoctoral associate at the Department of Bioengineering, Stanford University and at Google X, and a scientist at Verily Life Sciences, before joining Yonsei University in 2018 and Seoul National University in 2025.

Her group develops imaging systems whose optics and algorithms are designed together through physics-guided deep learning and learned optical design. Current work spans Fourier ptychographic microscopy and label-free virtual staining for pathology, ultra-thin lensless cameras with custom phase masks, and rolling-shutter speckle imaging of blood flow. She is an Associate Editor of Optics Express and Advanced Imaging, and received a 2026 Vice Prime Minister's commendation for contributions to science and technology.

Her professional service includes General Chair of the Computational Optical Sensing and Imaging (COSI) conference at the Optica Imaging Congress (2024, 2025), Conference Co-chair at SPIE Photonics West (2024–2026) and Director of International Affairs of the Optical Society of Korea. At Seoul National University she teaches Optical Physics and Design to undergraduate students and Computational Optical Imaging to graduate students.
